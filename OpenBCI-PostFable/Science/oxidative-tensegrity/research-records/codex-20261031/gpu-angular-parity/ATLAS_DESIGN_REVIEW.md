# Proposed 3D collagen architecture atlas — design review, not executed

The new angular component has device parity; that is not full validation of the FCC equilibrium/damage pipeline, nor validation of phantomized/stochastic generators. The proposed atlas is a sound next scientific direction after whole-solver and generator commissioning. The US$2 authorization in this task covers parity, not this atlas. No atlas simulation was launched.

## Exact screening count and contrasts

A defensible interpretation of 360 is 3 generators × 15 prescribed settings × 8 paired graph-realization blocks = 360 generator/settings/seed records. Running both fixed-microscopic-material (E1) and matched-initial-G_xy (E2) branches requires at least 720 mechanical evaluations, before load protocols, convergence checks or sizes. Forty-five conditions each with eight realizations also gives 360; these are not 360 independent graphs if the same graph is rotated or loaded in several directions.

Use 15 settings per generator: one baseline; two changes each in orientation distribution, loading direction, connectivity, tortuosity and crosslink placement (ten controlled one-factor contrasts); and four prespecified interactions. A proposed concrete matrix is in ATLAS_SCREENING_PLAN.csv (360 rows). This is a screening design with selected interactions, not a full five-factor factorial or a powered confirmatory study. It cannot estimate every interaction independently. Randomized execution order and paired block IDs are provided; do not treat pairing of unrelated generators by seed number as identical topology. Reuse the same underlying graph for true direction/rotation contrasts where the intervention permits it, and analyze those as repeated measures.

Baseline targets: common physical box, prescribed collagen volume fraction, target crosslink-node degree 3.2, approximately isotropic orientation, straight/rest geometry, and spatially uniform crosslinks. Connectivity contrasts 2.8/3.6 stay within the common sub-four range attainable by standard binary-junction phantom networks. Orientation order targets S=0.35/0.70, loading x/y/z, tortuosity 1.05/1.15, and clustered/gradient crosslink placement are proposed settings requiring generator feasibility tests. Four interactions: strongly aligned loaded y; strongly aligned loaded z; low connectivity with tortuosity 1.15; high connectivity with clustered links. These numeric targets are proposed design values, not measured collagen parameters.

## Generator and matching gates

Diluted FCC is a lattice control, not automatically a realistic collagen junction distribution. For phantom FCC, split co-located lattice intersections into independent binary fibre junctions while preserving fibre identity; geometric coincidence alone must not imply connectivity. This changes degree support, independent-node count and percolating pathways. Standard phantom FCC literature constructs z=4 junctions before dilution, so requesting z>4 across all generators would fail by construction. [Licup et al.](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4534289/) and [Elastic regimes of subisostatic athermal fiber networks](https://www.nat.vu.nl/~fcm/documents/PhysRevE.93.012407.pdf) document these choices.

In stochastic 3D, ideal zero-thickness rods almost never intersect exactly. Define a finite capture radius, crosslink insertion/connectors, distance/orientation criteria and fibre-length distribution; report capture-radius sensitivity. Do not obtain connectivity by quietly introducing different bond laws or prestress. Use common strain/rest-length and rest-angle definitions across generators. Curved fibres require appropriate stress-free rest angles and enough segments to resolve curvature; otherwise the curvature intervention also changes prestress or discretization.

Proposed primary matching gates, frozen before generation: physical box lengths identical; collagen volume fraction ±2%; realized mean crosslink-node degree within ±0.05 of target; crosslink-node count ±5%; prescribed crosslink count/stiffness/rule identical within each fixed-material comparison; order S within ±0.03 and tortuosity within ±0.02 where defined. Report full achieved distributions and all failures; these initial tolerances require feasibility review rather than post-outcome relaxation. Record degree including isolates, load-bearing component degree, fibre-contour-length density, segment lengths, junction valence, connected/spanning fractions and image winding separately.

Match collagen volume fraction using total fibre volume, not just node count or degree. Fibre cross section enters axial/bending stiffness; changing diameter to repair volume-fraction mismatch also changes microscopic mechanics. Prefer matching box, total contour length and fixed fibre cross section, allowing explicit node-count mismatches if necessary. Count degree-2 curvature-discretization nodes separately from physical crosslink nodes. A matching failure is an outcome, not grounds to relabel the generator. Cluster/gradient link placement should preserve total link count and, where feasible, stratify by degree so degree bias does not masquerade as spatial clustering. No molecular chemistry has been calibrated here; a common generic link law is a mechanical control.

## Observables and interpretation

Screening: full available tangent stiffness tensor, nonlinear directional stress–strain, resolved equilibrium residuals, accepted-step fraction, first damage, peak stress, failure strain and damage maps. Follow-up on a prespecified subset: notched crack extension, traction–separation, unloading/recoil, dissipated work and cyclic damage. Define crack connectivity/localization metrics and save element-level histories. Do not label global unnotched loading work a fracture energy. An incomplete/capped failure trace is censored, not a measured failure strain or toughness.

Purely elastic unbroken unloading is reversible and gives no genuine hysteretic dissipation or rate-dependent recovery. Current irreversible threshold damage can dissipate energy under loading, but does not create healing or validated time-dependent fatigue. A cyclic decline generated by that threshold rule is model-specific damage accumulation. Physical fatigue, solvent relaxation or recovery time requires the corresponding independently validated constitutive law.

Use paired graph-level contrasts, uncertainty over eight independent realization blocks and sensitivity to failed cases. Eight seeds are a screening choice, not evidence of adequate statistical power. Do not count time points, elements or E1/E2 branches as extra independent realizations. Add at least three box sizes on a prespecified representative subset with independent graph realizations and fixed physical density/normalized geometry; those runs are additional to 360. This is representative-volume/convergence testing; critical finite-size scaling needs a separate near-transition design.

Report Pareto sets separately by E1/E2 and loading direction, with uncertainty, between strength, a valid fracture observable and recoil. Recoil needs a declared definition, such as recovered strain fraction after unloading; energy release alone is not necessarily desirable. Pareto front membership should survive independent seeds/sizes and a confirmatory held-out set before calling a network optimal.

Benchmark the strongest effects against experiments with compatible collagen type, concentration, preparation, crosslinking, loading mode, strain/rate and stress definitions. Relevant starting points are [Licup et al.](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4534289/) for nonlinear collagen mechanics and [Sharma et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/1506.07792) for strain-controlled rigidity. These support study design; they do not already validate this atlas. Molecular aging and collagen–elastin coupling should wait until the baseline generator, normalization and mechanical observables pass their own gates.
