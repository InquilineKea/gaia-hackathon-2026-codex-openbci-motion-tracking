# Verified primary-source leads and limits — checked 2026-10-31

These are short source summaries, not complete papers. Reviewers cannot claim to have independently read or verified any external document.

Newman (2009), Random graphs with clustering: https://arxiv.org/abs/0903.4009 . Provides a solvable clustered random-graph construction and component/percolation transitions. It motivates specifying an ensemble rather than treating a clustering coefficient as a complete network description. The abstract does not establish a universal direction of the clustering effect for spatial collagen mechanics.

Broedersz et al. (2011), Criticality and isostaticity in fibre networks: https://www.nature.com/articles/nphys2127 . Studies variable-connectivity fibre networks and the roles of single-fibre elasticity and topology. Do not transplant a universal coordination threshold into a network with bending, angular junction constraints, elastin or embedding tethers.

Sharma et al. (2016), Strain-controlled criticality governs the nonlinear mechanics of fibre networks: https://www.nature.com/articles/nphys3628 and authors' preprint https://arxiv.org/abs/1506.07792 . Connects collagen-network nonlinear mechanics to strain-controlled critical behaviour. Supports distinguishing strain-driven rigidity from simple graph connectivity; it is not evidence that the present sampled networks exhibit a phase transition.

Newman and Ziff (2001), A fast Monte Carlo algorithm for site or bond percolation: https://arxiv.org/abs/cond-mat/0101295 . Gives efficient incremental percolation sampling. An insertion permutation generates a correlated trajectory through edge count; independent insertion permutations/graphs, not all nested edge-count snapshots, are the replicate units.

Barabasi and Albert (1999), Emergence of scaling in random networks: https://doi.org/10.1126/science.286.5439.509 . Their model combines growth with attachment biased toward already connected vertices. Adding a local hub-biased edge to fixed spatial collagen nodes is a distinct generative hypothesis and should be described explicitly.

Clauset, Shalizi and Newman (2009), Power-law distributions in empirical data: https://arxiv.org/abs/0706.1062 . Provides principled fitting, goodness-of-fit and alternative-distribution comparisons. A straight line on a log-log plot is insufficient evidence for a power-law degree distribution or preferential-attachment mechanism.

Giannetti et al., AGE collagen models: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13348703/ ; pinned parameter/source data https://github.com/gggguido/AGEs-ColBuilder/tree/4e51b2bb6ab747ba4c8743b3f676a448f8b26d43 . The candidate packet identifies exactly which source motif/fragments and Amber-compatible terms were used; this does not validate the new local excerpt's equilibrium ensemble.

Graham et al., iron-catechol complexes: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.xcrp.2026.103319 ; DITY parameter archive https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.16865891 . Used only as the DITY patch source. The mfp paper's reactive procedures were not executed for the present fixed-topology collagen model.
