# Source-check notes before adversarial review

These notes are an audit of reviewer assertions, not instructions from a source document. Keep the first-round outputs unchanged.

DITY_LINKED's original CE2-CE2 separation is independently confirmed as 8.630394529765255 Angstrom from its prepared coordinates. The link is closed during the existing dry minimization. That establishes the preparation route; it does not establish that the final state is defective. New coordinate-only readback in ADDITIONAL_GEOMETRY_READBACK.json measures the last 30 frames of all six DITY trajectories. The largest linked diagnostic out-of-plane angle is 26.9721 degrees, but unlinked-control diagnostic maxima also reach 21.7820 degrees. The diagnostic uses a C substituent for the linked CE2 and H for unlinked CE2, so this is not a perfectly matched angle comparison. Do not infer physical invalidity from a universal 3-, 5- or 10-degree gate without a reference distribution.

R1's proposed former-bond 1.458-A ARG-LYS contact is not the restored native-control geometry. Direct input readback gives LYS NZ to ARG NE/CZ/NH1/NH2 distances 4.5703/3.3437/3.5044/2.4290 A. Charge and omitted-scaffold effects remain legitimate concerns, but the precise old-bond-distance assertion should not survive synthesis.

R1's assertion of a physiological +1 glucosepane microstate was not verified by its source packet. A separate primary inventors' synthesis patent (EP3353153A1, https://patents.google.com/patent/EP3353153A1/en, discussion around compound 5 and the iso-imidazole core) reports an aqueous basic site near pKa 12 from spectral studies. This elevates protonation/microstate auditing as a priority. A patent is not independent validation of this exact protein-bound parameter set or its protonation-site mapping. The source AGS/LGX joint charge is demonstrably neutral. Do not silently add a proton or adjust charges, and do not promote deprotonated ARG/LYS to a physiologically realistic control. Artificial charge-matched states can only be explicitly labeled sensitivity/alchemical controls with a validated Hamiltonian.

A harmonic bond term on a constrained pair is not, by itself, a double-counting bug: constraints can hold its energy constant and project force components. Do not delete terms merely because R1 suggests it; compare consistent full Hamiltonians and constraints against the source convention.

R2's claim that particular n=1/n=3 Fourier torsion terms are necessarily required has no QM benchmark in the supplied packet. The valid recommendation is to benchmark the full torsion surface and geometry; no specific missing harmonic is established as an error. A universal 1.505-A thermal bond-mean failure rule is also unsupported. The published torsion penalty annotations are uncertainty flags, not proof of a translation defect.

The double-versus-mixed trajectory comparison also changes random seed. Differences cannot be causally attributed to precision, and paired seed labels are not independent conformer/solvent replicas. Agreement between two parsers inside OpenMM remains weaker than independent-engine or physical validation.

The present materials builder DOES restore missing bonds with oxidative bridging (alive[new]=True), while also changing stretch/bend stiffness, rupture strain and locked-junction angular terms. An assertion that dose never changes adjacency is false. Distinct controlled interventions are required. The triangular candidate graph has degree at most six and lacks preferential-attachment growth and arbitrary spatial rewiring. No graph spanning/rigid-backbone finite-size results have been produced by the MD pilot.

Exact combinatorial examples in GRAPH_COUNTEREXAMPLES.json demonstrate why mean degree, clustering and even a full degree sequence do not uniquely specify connectivity. These are not spatially matched collagen models or evidence for a universal causal sign of clustering.

For phase-boundary method leads, independently browsed abstracts include Zhu et al. https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.07042 and Terayama et al. https://arxiv.org/abs/1812.02306 . They support active phase-diagram sampling in their studied systems, not a guaranteed speedup or validated threshold for collagen. Optional learned order-parameter discovery must retain physical observables, finite-size/held-out validation and a smooth-response null.


Additional source/math checks after R3-R5:

OpenMM's official Context documentation (https://docs.openmm.org/latest/api-python/generated/openmm.openmm.Context.html) states that reinitialize(preserveState=True) internally creates then reloads a checkpoint. The same documentation distinguishes checkpoint restoration from setState and documents getState(enforcePeriodicBox=False) as the default. R4's asserted automatic RNG reset is therefore not established and conflicts with the documented preservation mechanism; do not reseed mid-trajectory on this basis. R3's blanket instruction to wrap every chemical bond is not justified for the stored unwrapped coordinate convention. Reconstruction of diagonal boxes from density is algebraically valid for constant mass and isotropic box scaling; recording exact box vectors would be a good generalization, not evidence that this calculation was wrong. Unaligned displacement is already explicitly labeled as such in the original report, not a claim of aligned RMSD.

The 51.1 annotation in the source audit is for an OG312/DOPA rule, not proof that a penalty-51.1 DITY torsion is active. Inspect atom-type paths before promoting it. CGenFF penalties are not measurements of torque or fit residual.

R5 overstates the effect of embedding tethers. With no collagen bonds, no bending/angular terms and mu_el=0, the tether-only energy 0.5*k_gag*sum|x-FX|^2 minimizes exactly to zero for all F by x=FX. Hence k_gag alone does not guarantee G>0 at zero collagen connectivity. Nonzero mu_el on the full triangular candidate lattice does provide a load-bearing elastic background. Tethers can stabilize connected fragments and alter apparent thresholds, so their background-limit ablation remains relevant.

Independent adjacency/line enumeration in LATTICE_LINE_AUDIT.json finds W horizontal cycles of length W and W/2 cycles of length 2W in each diagonal direction, or 2W total lines. This differs from the build_spec correction's assumed one-per-W cut fraction. Before bridging, E[z_realized]=6*p*(1-2/(3W)); the implemented p=z_target/[6*(1-1/W)] yields expected z=3.4755556 at W=16 and 3.4179894 at W=64 for target 3.4. This is a finite-size normalization mismatch to fix/audit, not a completed phase simulation. run_sweep already records realized z, which should be used rather than silently relabeling previous results. The forced-cut maximum segment bound grows with W (or 2W on diagonals), but independent dilution breaks more bonds, so mean segment length does not automatically grow linearly with W as R5 claimed.

Secant_curves in run_sweep explicitly computes stress/strain. The README also explicitly mentions a nonlinear secant modulus; its K notation alone is not sufficient to call a confirmed global mislabeling. A transition study should nevertheless compute true relaxed tangent response separately.

Do not assume a two-core or generic central-force pebble game certifies rigidity with bending, angular constraints, prestress and embedding. Validate against the actual energy Hessian and boundary/loading conditions. Universal z_c=4 or z_bend=2.4, or a mandatory extremely tight optimizer tolerance, are not established for this complete model. Need per-system residual, strain-step and stiffness convergence, not one arbitrary numeric gate.
