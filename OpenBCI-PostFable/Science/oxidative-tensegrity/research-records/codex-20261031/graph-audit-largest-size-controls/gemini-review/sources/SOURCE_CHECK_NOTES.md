# Claim checks for graph audit and largest-size controls

## Fixed interpretation before critique

The graph generator is AST-identical to the prior executed rate-control source. Successful reconstruction requires each mask hash, saved graph aggregates, total bending count and triangle-arm degree sequence to match. Active-edge indices, exact degrees, component labels and per-component winding vectors/ranks now permit direct checks of hypotheses previously inferred from aggregates. Graph reconstruction does not add specimens or prove chain mixing. Degree support0..6 follows the lattice; an observed histogram can establish a finite-graph distribution, not a thermodynamic phase or a growing preferential-attachment law.

Per-component winding-vector rank is an exact integer linear-span rank of cycle winding vectors. Primitive normalization preserves rank but does not record the full integer homology subgroup index. Rank is not biconnectivity, full mechanical rigidity or a universal percolation threshold. Old directional flags are valid declared indicators; a difference from rank2 diagnoses an interpretation limit, not necessarily an old implementation bug. The giant component is the first root encountered among equal-sized components. Exact giant degree is2*giant_edges/giant_nodes; z divided by giant fraction assumes all edges lie in that component and can be wrong.

Largest-size control matrix has384 unique new mechanical cases:288 time-step cases plus144 designated rate cases with48 overlaps. Same four L24 seeds, three coordination values, all four arms for time-step checks, only uniform/hub arms for extra rate checks. These are additional numerical controls of the same old graph specimens, not384 independent networks or new replication. Matched-stiffness gate is G0>1e-6 plus numerical completeness and small-strain consistency; ineligible does not mean a failed solve.

The predeclared .05->.025 adequacy tolerance is absolute retention difference<=.0001, or0.01 percentage points, on eligible cases. Passing it concerns this observable at tested settings only, not all trajectories, the dt->0 limit or physical correctness. A failure must be reported and small topology differences interpreted against the observed error. Test contrast stability within the same seed and time step; do not compare an error from a different cell as a bound on a particular effect. Constant-rate controls at dt.1 have no new half/quarter-step cases in this matrix, so do not attribute fine-step accuracy to them without qualification.

The logged maximum uncapped argument is beta*abs(e-q) before each memory update; cap counts measure values>=30. An observed count0 establishes inactivity at sampled update states in these runs, not universal absence between updates or elsewhere. The log does not measure molecular bond forces or reaction kinetics. Whole-energy stiffness normalization remains a compensation of microscopic stiffness; changing to force-activated memory would define a different law.

The source supplies two analytic homogeneous references: exp(-5) is fully relaxing; r+(1-r)*exp(-5) is equal equilibrium fraction. For kappa0,beta0,r.5 the latter applies to eligible finite-stiffness networks. None of the new work calibrates collagen chemistry, physical time, biological aging, nonlinear strain stiffening or a physical phase transition. Four paired seed values support descriptive comparisons only. Same-model reviewers are internal checks, not independent experimental validation.
