## Specialist adjudication before adversarial review

R1_graphs correctly emphasizes exact reconstruction, distinction between winding and shear response, giant-density/bending covariation and weak clustering separation. Its claim that degree-preserving triangle rewiring eliminates component-size confounding is false in general: it preserves degrees and isolates, but can alter component connectivity; the prior saved largest-component values at coordination2.4 differ among triangle arms. Its phrase that an intermediate-degree histogram falsifies a stationary thermodynamic phase overstates the result: the observed histograms refute a pure0/6-only distribution in these finite graphs; stationarity and thermodynamics are untested. No geometric characterization as a dense triangulated ribbon is established from the aggregate tables alone.

R1's suggested scaling by tiny triangle/bend differences does not identify causal derivatives, and generic degree-preserving2-switch rewiring must respect nearest-neighbor lattice geometry, directional edge counts, component constraints and proposal probabilities. Otherwise it changes the model or ensemble. Feasibility of all these simultaneous constraints is not established. Shared old seed blocks do not add independent replication.

R2_numerics correctly notes the late-time versus transient distinction, per-case versus paired-contrast error, positive-G0 conditioning and redundant normalized stress. Its claimed universal bound of about0.0034 on rate sensitivity from0.5*exp(-5) is unsupported for the coupled bending/axial-memory model. Quasistatic displacement changes the edge extensions as memory relaxes; coupled modes need not have the homogeneous unit-rate decay. A maximum sampled rate factor below2.85 does not establish a universal stress-tail bound or unique static energy-partition mechanism. Existing-trace analysis now gives direct evidence: at L24,z4.8,kappa.003,t.5, mean beta10-minus-beta0 normalized stress is−0.04730885 uniform and−0.05542922 hub, while final differences are much smaller. The transient table uses dt.1 only and is descriptive.

Richardson extrapolation would be assumption-dependent, not a required replacement of measured values; no extrapolated limit is reported as established. The1.07e-4 figure is the largest .05->.025 paired-contrast change, not a bound on .1->.025 drift. The latter maximum individual-case difference is2.93849e-4. No residual/error estimate proves exact numerical correctness.

Both specialists overstate rate-law invariance or a uniquely demonstrated stretching/bending partition mechanism. A generic denser uniform arm changes whole-network edge budget and degree distribution, so matching one giant-degree mean alone is a useful sensitivity comparison, not a direct causal separation of every mechanism. Triangle/bending covariation and spatial arrangement still require control.
