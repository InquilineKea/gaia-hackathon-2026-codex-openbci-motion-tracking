# Source and mathematical adjudication notes

These are additional checks; original Gemini outputs remain unchanged and fallible.

All 1,344 production cases converged. The 760 cases excluded from matched-stiffness comparisons fall at or below the operational G0 gate; none was excluded for failed CG convergence. Changing the G0 gate to 1e-8 retains the same 584 eligible production cases; raising it to 1e-4 retains 582. The largest-size z4.8 illustrative contrasts retain all four pairs and the same means under all three gates. Unconditioned stiffness and connectivity are different questions from conditional matched-stiffness relaxation.

The winding booleans intentionally mean any nonzero winding coefficient along each oblique lattice basis direction, aggregated across components. A diagonal cycle can set both. They are not a winding-rank measurement, full 2D rigidity proof, or graph biconnectivity test. Computing winding-vector rank per component is a useful extension, but calling rank two 'biconnected wrapping' confuses distinct graph/topology definitions.

The susceptibility field is explicitly mean finite-cluster size sum(s^2)/sum(s), excluding the largest component and including isolated nodes. This is a valid convention, not by itself an implementation artifact. When all remaining components are isolates its value is one, consistent with their size; it does not measure the fraction of disconnected vertices. A volume-normalized second moment, isolated-node fraction and component count would provide complementary information, not a mandatory correction. Those unrecorded statistics cannot be recovered from current aggregates without graph reconstruction.

Source invariants check degree sequences for triangle interventions and directional edge counts for all arms. Bending motifs can change. Their counts are already saved per case and in paired-contrasts.csv; graph-supplement.csv now exposes them in aggregate too. Low achieved triangle contrast cannot establish that clustering is unimportant. Indexed rejection-free hexagon proposals would need the correct proposal-probability ratio or time weighting to preserve the intended target measure; do not simply replace uniform-center proposals and retain an unchanged Metropolis acceptance formula.

Hub bias is a finite-step fixed-node edge-replacement intervention at bounded maximum degree six. It co-changes clustering and fragmentation; it does not identify an isolated degree-variance effect or a growing preferential-attachment mechanism. Finite-size differences from three sizes and four seeds do not prove sampler non-convergence, thermodynamic condensation or universality. No stationary-ensemble mixing claim is made.

Reusing seeds across z is a valid common-random-number block structure if uncertainty respects it. It is not automatically a replication defect and independent seeds at every z are not mandatory. There are four seed replicates per setting, 12 size-seed blocks, eight distinct seed IDs, 84 base graph settings and 336 treatment variants, with 311 distinct masks. Time points and constitutive/control repeats are not independent graph specimens. Intervals are pointwise exploratory bootstrap intervals over at most four pairs, not multiplicity-adjusted or powered equivalence tests.

In this linear geometry, initial stress/G0 equals the prescribed .1 shear amplitude, so matched_final_stress is .1 times retention up to numerical error. These are redundant observables, not independent confirmations. The equal-fraction homogeneous-energy null is r+(1-r)*exp(-5). The runtime's exp(-5) field is an additional fully relaxing analytic reference; both definitions are explicit in ACTUAL_DESIGN and derived case tables. No numerical homogeneous-null trajectory is claimed.

The CPU model intentionally linearizes geometry, has no damage and has no calibrated physical units. It cannot reproduce nonlinear finite-strain criticality or H100 fracture findings. Its positive-directional-G0 gate does not prove full rigidity or constrain all strain modes. Absolute source force residuals, exact linearity and local finite-difference tests do not alone establish error bounds on extremely soft or physical models. Time-step refinement covers only 16 width12 cases and cannot bound the error of the entire grid.

The preliminary benchmark source was modified before exact production/control snapshots were retained; this is disclosed in PROVENANCE_NOTES. The initial control launch failed before numerical execution because its namespace wrapper was omitted. The corrected control launch and all completed results are retained; no shared VM was stopped.


R3 additional checks: Degree-squared bias demonstrably increases degree variance and clustering, with lower largest-component fractions in sampled settings. Aggregate transitivity near the triangular-lattice limit is consistent with dense patches, but does not prove a stationary condensation phase, an exact k=0/k=6 degree distribution, or absence of relative degree hubs. The generator is deliberately bounded fixed-node degree bias, not a scale-free network.

The heuristic z/S_largest equals largest-component mean degree only if all edges belong to that component; this is not established from every aggregate row. Ratios of group means also need care. Do not call this a measured subgraph coordination. Whole-area stress normalization is the correct declared bulk observable, not an error. Renormalizing by a fixed active area cannot change stress retention or stress/G0 because the area factor cancels. Such a change could define a different raw-modulus question but cannot explain a retention difference algebraically.

Small mean-clustering contrasts and global-transitivity differences should be reported next to effect sizes, not divided into an allegedly causal derivative. Near-zero denominator contrasts and co-changing motifs can make those ratios unstable. Defining degree<2 local clustering as zero and including all nodes is a valid explicitly stated convention; a conditional active-node clustering statistic is complementary rather than a correction.


R4/R5 checks: Matched final stress is redundant with retention in this linear strain-controlled model, as already documented. Force-activated memory is a different physical hypothesis; changing to it is not a repair. The analytic homogeneous null uses unit rate while selective memory uses strain acceleration, so their difference cannot uniquely isolate bending selectivity. Targeted constant-rate and relaxing central-force controls were commissioned within the original deadline to address this at the largest high-density setting.

A compatible semidefinite equilibrium system can be solved in its range without arbitrary ground springs. Small force residuals do not bound nullspace displacements, which is why displacement nonaffinity is not used as a headline. Pinning one node per component is only a translation gauge if it removes translations without constraining physical deformations; arbitrary extra constraints must not be added as a blanket solver fix. No observed displacement drift is established by the saved aggregates.

The exp(30) rate cap is explicit in source; its activation was not logged, so no claim about whether it activated is justified. The model and source already describe the frozen-geometry splitting, but the cap should be stated explicitly. Half-step controls cover the listed 16 cases only.

Four-pair percentile bootstrap intervals are exploratory resampling summaries with unreliable nominal coverage, not confirmatory 95% evidence. Under the assumptions of an exact two-sided sign-flip test, four pairs cannot yield p<.05. A universal 12.5% false-positive floor is not inferred without the required symmetric independent-null assumptions. Cryptographic seeds are not necessary; seeded scientific PRNG streams and correct blocking are sufficient. The main synthesis should show the four paired values/ranges and avoid declaring significance from these intervals. UNCONDITIONED_STIFFNESS.json supplies all-pair raw G0 differences at z3.2, complementing the conditional matched-normalization estimand.
