# Primary evidence checked for the follow-up

These are short source summaries checked by Codex, not new experiments or exhaustive systematic reviews. The reviewers have no browsing tools. Evaluate the limits of each comparison and mark any additional literature suggestion unverified.

## H5 mechanotransduction context

Teuscher and colleagues, Nature Communications 2024, PMID 38177158, DOI 10.1038/s41467-023-44409-2. The abstract reports collagen dynamics and a hemidesmosome-linked mechanical feedback route involving YAP-1 in C. elegans longevity interventions. It supports a biological context for ECM mechanotransduction. It does not establish this project's numerical recovery-rate model or an individual remaining-lifespan predictor.
Primary source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38177158/

## H5 resilience analogy

Pyrkov and colleagues, Nature Communications 2021, PMID 34035236, DOI 10.1038/s41467-021-23014-1. The study analyzes longitudinal blood-marker fluctuations and physical-activity records to estimate organism-level recovery properties. It motivates studying recovery dynamics, but it does not measure a collagen–YAP–myosin loop in worms or validate this model's 6-hour timescale. Treat transfer to H5 as a hypothesis.
Primary source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34035236/

## H8 relevant negative evidence and its boundary

Reigle and colleagues, Journal of Cellular Biochemistry 2008, PMID 18348167, DOI 10.1002/jcb.21735. Their glucose-glycated type I collagen showed weaker heparin and keratan-sulfate proteoglycan binding, while decorin and biglycan bound similarly to normal and glycated collagen. Methods use D-glucose exposure, not a site-specific MG-H1-versus-CML comparison. This challenges a universal claim that glycation detaches all GAG/proteoglycan interfaces, but does not directly falsify a specific MG-H1 mechanism. Distinguish free DS chain binding, decorin core-protein binding and intact proteoglycan binding.
Primary source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18348167/
Methods and results: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2746388/

## Energy decomposition uncertainty

NVIDIA's AIMNet2/Ewald example describes a short-range ML energy plus separately evaluated electrostatics using predicted charges. It shows why adding a separate electrostatic component does not alone prove double counting. It does not establish the behavior of the exact pinned wrapper/checkpoint or validate a screened-solvent correction. Do not turn the earlier reviewer assertion into a confirmed bug.
Primary documentation: https://nvidia.github.io/nvalchemi-toolkit/examples/advanced/08_aimnet2_ewald_pipeline.html
