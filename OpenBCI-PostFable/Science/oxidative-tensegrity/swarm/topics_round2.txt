Hypothesis H1 (turnover escape): oxidative crosslinks make collagen protease-resistant so crosslinked molecules leave the turnover pool, and longevity interventions keep matrix renewable. Context: Teuscher et al. Nat Commun 2024 (C. elegans EMB-9 collagen IV layers on old un-turned-over matrix; daf-2 slows collagen stiffening). Find supporting and contradicting evidence (MMP resistance of AGE-collagen, collagen residence time vs crosslink load) and name the decisive pulse-chase experiment.
Hypothesis H2: YAP-1 mechanosensing reads the strain-stiffening (nonlinear) range of the matrix rather than its linear stiffness, and oxidative crosslinks flatten that range first. Context: Teuscher et al. Nat Commun 2024 (hemidesmosome-linked YAP-1 loop required for daf-2 longevity; pressure raises yap-1). Find evidence on cell responses to strain-stiffening hydrogels at matched stiffness and on glycation reducing collagen nonlinearity.
Hypothesis H3: dityrosine (BLI-3/DUOX peroxidase) and sulfilimine (peroxidasin) collagen crosslinks are required during development but harmful when they continue on adult matrix that is no longer renewed. Find evidence on bli-3, mlt-7, pxn-2 in C. elegans cuticle and basement membrane, adult-onset knockdown effects on lifespan or stiffness, and dityrosine accumulation with age.
Hypothesis H4: oxidative and glycation crosslinks accumulate preferentially on tensioned collagen fibrils (strain protects from collagenase), making tissues anisotropically stiff and brittle along the load axis. Find evidence on strain-dependent collagenase protection, load-dependent glycation, and age-related anisotropy and failure strain in tendon and artery.
Hypothesis H5: the recovery rate of the matrix-YAP-myosin feedback loop after a mechanical perturbation slows with age (critical slowing down) and predicts remaining lifespan in individual animals, analogous to Pyrkov and Fedichev's organism-level recovery rate. Find evidence on mechanotransduction recovery kinetics with age and on individual-level predictors of lifespan in C. elegans.
Hypothesis H6: YAP drives LOX/LOXL2 expression so stiff matrix stiffens itself; anti-crosslink therapy (alagebrium, aminoguanidine) should synergize with collagen renewal boosters (Gly:Pro:Hyp 3:1:1 per Dakhovnik et al. npj Aging 2025, col-120 activation). Verify whether YAP/TAZ directly regulate LOX or LOXL2, and find evidence on combining crosslink breakers with collagen synthesis.
